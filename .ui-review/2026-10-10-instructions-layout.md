# Landing instructions layout review

## Scope

- Landing page hero, CTA, contribution graph, and keyboard instructions.
- Reviewed at 1280 × 900 and 390 × 844.

## Result

- The keyboard instructions now sit beneath the contribution graph and its legend, keeping gameplay guidance attached to the playable area.
- The hero group is lowered slightly without disrupting the card platform or character movement space.
- The contribution card remains readable at both widths; the instructions wrap to two centered lines on mobile.
- No horizontal overflow was found.
- Lime lint and the production build pass.

## Findings

No blocking, high, or medium-severity issues found. The card's overlap with the ground strip on mobile is intentional and preserves the platform effect.
